For mills, garment makers, integrated groups and traders

Which European climate rules actually reach your textile business?

Buyers send questionnaires that mix law, contract terms and voluntary schemes. This portal keeps them apart, so you know what binds you, what a customer is asking as a commercial matter, and what your emissions inventory can and cannot prove.

Accounting

How you measure emissions

GHG Protocol boundaries, scopes, data coverage and factors. Voluntary unless a law, contract or scheme requires it. A limited Scope 1 and 2 inventory is not a full Scope 3 inventory, and neither is a product footprint.

Open the inventory tools
Legal applicability

Whether a rule binds you

CSRD, CSDDD, CBAM and the EU ecodesign framework each depend on the entity, turnover, goods and dates. Selling to an EU buyer does not by itself make you subject to their duties.

Screen your obligations
Commercial requirements

What a customer asks for

Buyer contracts, supplier portals, CDP, SBTi or Higg requests. These can apply even when no law binds you, and each has its own version and deadline.

Check a request or claim

A sensible route through the portal

  1. Screen what applies. Enter your entity, EU sales and goods. You get a status per instrument and the checks still needed.
  2. Decode the buyer request. Find out whether it is law, contract or a voluntary scheme, and what to prepare.
  3. Map sources and Scope 3. Work through all 15 Scope 3 categories and track the evidence you hold.
  4. Calculate with a traceable trail. Unit-checked lines, your own sourced factors, gross results by scope.
  5. Label and write up. Draft, screening estimate, reviewed or externally assured, based on the evidence you actually have.
What this portal will not do. It does not certify anything, does not produce a GHG Protocol certificate and never returns a blanket "EU compliant" result. It supplies no emission factors: you enter factors from sources you can cite. Chemical restrictions, fibre labelling, packaging, waste, consumer claims and labour rules are outside it.
Your data stays here. Everything you enter is kept in this browser only. Nothing is sent anywhere. Use the copy buttons to take results into your own files.

    Limits to keep in mind
    
            
      
              
    • CSRD and CSDDD thresholds shown are triage indicators from institutional summaries. Exact tests, aggregation rules, reference years and national implementation need the operative text.
      • 
              
    • Directive (EU) 2026/470 was not read in full when the snapshot was prepared. Its details come from the Council and Commission summaries.
      • 
              
    • The portal does not verify all current ESRS amendments, national laws or customs codes for your company.
      • 
              
    • UK, Switzerland and other European markets have their own law and are not screened here.
      • 
              
    • A planned or proposed measure is not an enacted requirement. This includes the planned DPP textile act, any proposed CBAM scope expansion, and the GHG Protocol and ISO consolidated standard.
      • 
              
    • A GHG inventory alone does not show compliance with CSRD, CSDDD, CBAM or product rules.

    Keeping a verification log
    
            
    Before a legal or public conformance conclusion, re-check the sources and record, for each: source ID or new source, exact title and version, publication and effective dates, accessed date, URL, article or table, the narrow claim it supports, adopted or proposed status, company facts used, uncertainty and reviewer. Replace stale conclusions explicitly instead of mixing old thresholds with new application dates.
